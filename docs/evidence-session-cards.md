## What each instruction means

**Batter**

- `take` -- Do not offer at any pitch this PA.
- `slap` -- Swing every pitch with NO charge.
- `charge_short` -- Charge, then release quickly (well under half the meter).
- `charge_medium` -- Charge to about a full meter (~1 s), then swing.
- `charge_long` -- Hold the charge 2 s or more, then swing.
- `charge_early` -- Charge, and deliberately swing EARLY for the pitch.
- `charge_late` -- Charge, and deliberately swing LATE for the pitch.
- `bunt` -- Bunt every pitch.
- `bunt_pull_back` -- Square to bunt, then pull back (do not offer).
- `star_swing` -- Star swing if the meter allows; otherwise slap and annotate star_unavailable.

**Pitcher**

- `normal` -- Plain pitch, no charge.
- `changeup` -- Changeup, no charge.
- `charge_short` -- Charge briefly, then throw.
- `charge_medium` -- Charge about half-way, then throw.
- `charge_long` -- Charge as long as possible, then throw.
- `release_early` -- Throw the moment you can (fast release).
- `release_late` -- Wait as long as you can before throwing (slow release).
- `star` -- Star pitch if the meter allows; otherwise plain and annotate star_unavailable.

**Aim**

- `middle` -- Aim down the middle.
- `left_edge` -- Aim at the LEFT edge of the zone (3B side on screen).
- `right_edge` -- Aim at the RIGHT edge of the zone (1B side on screen).
- `outside_left` -- Aim clearly OUTSIDE, left.
- `outside_right` -- Aim clearly OUTSIDE, right.

**Fielding**

- `control_no_special` -- Field normally. No dive, jump, Buddy action or manual selection change.
- `dive_every_chance` -- Dive at every ball you can reach.
- `jump_every_chance` -- Jump at every ball you can reach (wall balls and liners).
- `miss_dive` -- On every ball in play, press DIVE once with a fielder nowhere near the ball.
- `miss_jump` -- On every ball in play, press JUMP once with a fielder nowhere near the ball.
- `buddy_attack` -- Buddy Attack whenever possible, including ones that will miss.
- `buddy_jump` -- Buddy Jump on every high ball near the wall, even hopeless ones.
- `buddy_throw` -- Attempt a Buddy Throw on every throw where one is offered.
- `selection_change` -- Switch the controlled fielder at least twice on every ball in play.

**Running**

- `no_shake` -- Do not shake at all while runners move.
- `short_shake` -- One short shake burst (under 1 s) at contact, then stop.
- `sustained_shake` -- Shake continuously from contact until every runner stops.
- `delayed_shake` -- Wait ~1 s after contact, then shake until runners stop.
