## Remaining review items

Three additional ordinary failed contacts were detected at frames `34833`,
`41685`, and `98808`, but their human notes were lost. They remain useful as
failed fielding opportunities for OAA; they are not accepted as official errors.

The current airborne byte was false near all six ordinary failed contacts in
this session. It therefore cannot, by itself, separate a routine boot from a
failed dive. Official error automation should eventually use the expected-out
probability plus detected special effects, not the rule “bobble + safe batter =
error.” Until that model is validated, error calls on failed contacts require
review while OAA uses the out/no-out result directly.
