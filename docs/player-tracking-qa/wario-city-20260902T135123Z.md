## Fixes established by this game

1. **PA 63 joins on outs.** Light Blue Yoshi also batted at 0-0 in the top of the seventh on PA
   53. The joiner used inning, half, batter and count but omitted the out count already present in
   both feeds, so both PA 53 and PA 63 were candidates. PA 53 is now identified at zero outs and
   PA 63 at two outs.
2. **PA 84's second fair ball was a replay.** The game replayed the same Blue Yoshi contact 559
   frames later with identical contact, landing, pitch lead and swing lead, but cut the live phase
   after 0.7341 seconds. That left only three quarter-second trajectory samples before reset,
   shorter than the old five-sample threshold. Subsecond copies now require those three samples
   plus all four exact event landmarks; divergent balls still survive.
3. **False bobbles are discarded.** The executable's shared animation line named a bobble on PAs
   55, 57, 62, 70 and 90, while `last_contact_fielder` proved the named fielder never touched the
   ball. These are no longer exposed as bobbles or warnings. The raw name and no-contact reason
   remain as discarded audit evidence. Confirmed physical bobbles, egg/fireball contacts and
   extraordinary-effort error vetoes are unchanged.

## Stadium-gimmick decision

Wario City confirms that trajectory discontinuities and player stuns are consequences, not stable
cause labels. Prior Peach Ice Garden and DK Jungle captures already provide the same counterexample
for Freezies and flowers. Because the requested feature must individually identify every event in
all nine stadiums, stadium-event output remains disabled rather than shipping a partial arrow/pipe
heuristic. The complete researched event inventory and the evidence gate are maintained in
`docs/tracker-validation-console.md`.
