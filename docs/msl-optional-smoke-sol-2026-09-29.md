## Reproduced defect and fix

Before the fix, a rapid **Admin → Edit Season → clear Season name → Save Season** after navigating through the site sent `PATCH /rest/v1/seasons?id=eq.7` despite the textbox being empty. The editor was mounted while `season` was `null`; its effect then copied the season into form state after the user's edit and restored the old name. The initial attempted change to the effect dependency did not close the race. The final change mounts `EditSeasonModal` only when a season is selected, with its season ID as the key, so the form starts from the selected season synchronously and resets when switching seasons. Repeating the exact rapid sequence on the rebuilt app at 1280 and 390 px produced the required-name error and **zero** season writes. The final full browser pass and `npm.cmd run build` passed. The fix is local and has not been deployed by this pass.
